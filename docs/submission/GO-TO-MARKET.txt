GO-TO-MARKET AND USER ACQUISITION

WHO THE FIRST USERS ARE
People who already hold a little MON or USDC on Monad, are curious about earning from the order book, and find it opaque. Three groups:
1. Monad newcomers and crypto-curious builders who have used a swap and want a next step that is not leverage.
2. Chinese-speaking Web3 beginners, reached directly in their own language through the communities where they already ask questions.
3. People who like game-like products and want to plant a $10 to $20 garden, compete on the leaderboard and unlock achievements.

WHY THEY WOULD CARE
- A first action that costs about $5 to $20, and no browser wallet to install: a passkey (Face ID or Touch ID) is enough.
- Real fills visible within seconds, and a ledger that shows losses as clearly as gains.
- A preview, a simulation and one confirmation before every transaction; tools to prune or move orders when inventory drifts; and a small hedge on Perpl for the MON that flip orders accumulate.
- A leaderboard ranked by completed round trips and garden age, never by profit.

MARKET CONTEXT (dated, sources opened)
- Liquidity-management products earn fees on other chains. DefiLlama 30-day fees, read on 2026-10-10: Snuggle about $1.70M, HawkFi about $0.92M, Arrakis Modular about $0.52M, Kamino Liquidity about $0.20M.
- On Monad, DefiLlama lists no third-party product in this category (same date). Kuru's central limit order book traded about $1.42M in 24 hours against about $33.9M for all Monad DEXs (DefiLlama, 2026-10-10).
- Kuru ships its own liquidity interface and vaults (docs.kuru.io, read 2026-10-10). Flip Garden is a budget-first, game-like entry point with a ledger for the beginner.

CHANNELS, IN ORDER
1. Demos with real fills: a short clip of a real flip order filling and flipping on Monad, with the transaction hash visible, posted where Monad and Kuru builders already are (X, Discord) and in Chinese-language communities.
2. Open source: a public repository with real-data tests and a findings file that links every transaction.
3. Direct outreach: invite 20 to 50 testers from the Monad builder circles to run a $10 to $20 garden and collect their feedback.
4. Ecosystem: ask Kuru and Monad community leads for feedback and for a pointer from their docs to a beginner front end.

HOW SUCCESS IS MEASURED
- Activation: a tester plants a first garden and sees a first fill within the first session.
- Understanding: a tester can explain in their own words why their number went up or down.
- Retention: they come back within seven days to look at or adjust their garden.
- Trust: no unexplained difference between the ledger and on-chain balances.

BUSINESS MODEL
The first version does not charge. Later options: a small integrator fee on trades routed through Kuru's aggregator interface, or paid extras for power users.
