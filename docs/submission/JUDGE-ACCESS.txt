HOW TO TRY FLIP GARDEN (no account or login is needed to look around)

1. Open https://owenzhao9.github.io/flip-garden/ in any modern browser, on a phone or a computer.

2. Ledger tab (read-only, no wallet needed): press "Try the example wallet". It re-reads our own real-money test from Monad mainnet (blocks 112,209,462 to 112,211,600; about 15 to 25 seconds the first time) and shows "Matches the chain" with all 6 checks at a difference of 0, a cash change of -4.976961 USDC, a coin change of +201 MON and network fees of 0.095135 MON. You can also paste any Monad wallet address and a start block (the first block to read) to see that wallet's Kuru flip-order ledger and its garden: growth stages, level and achievements.

3. Board tab (read-only): the leaderboard, ranked by completed round trips and garden age, never by profit. Anyone can join with a public wallet address and a start block; the service recomputes every figure from public chain data.

4. Plant, Hedge and Passkey tabs (they use real money; a judge does not need them): Plant connects a browser wallet or a passkey session on Monad mainnet and previews up to three flip bids, with a simulation and a single confirmation per transaction; approvals are for the exact amount and the hard caps are 15 USDC per deposit and per planting. Hedge opens and closes a small short on Perpl's MON perpetual (caps: 15 USD of hedge, 15 AUSD deposited). Passkey signs in with Face ID or Touch ID (needs Safari with iCloud Keychain, or Chrome with Google Password Manager) and swaps a few dollars of USDC into AUSD. Use a few dollars.

5. Source code, 226 tests, every real transaction hash (docs/FINDINGS.md): https://github.com/OwenZhao9/flip-garden
