HOW TO TRY FLIP GARDEN (no account or login is needed to look around)

1. Open https://owenzhao9.github.io/flip-garden/ in any modern browser, on a phone or a computer.

2. Ledger tab (read-only, no wallet needed): press "Try the example wallet". It re-reads our own real-money test from Monad mainnet (blocks 112,209,462 to 112,211,600; about 15 to 25 seconds the first time) and shows "Matches the chain" with all 6 checks at a difference of 0, a cash change of -4.976961 USDC, a coin change of +201 MON and network fees of 0.095135 MON. You can also paste any Monad wallet address and a start block (the first block to read) to see that wallet's Kuru flip-order ledger and its garden: 3D growth stages, level and achievements.

3. Board tab (read-only): the leaderboard, ranked by completed round trips and garden age, never by profit. Anyone can join with a public wallet address and a start block; the service recomputes every figure from public chain data.

4. The signed-in flow without money: open https://owenzhao9.github.io/flip-garden/demo.html . It is the same app running against a simulated Monad chain, market and passkey (a banner says so). Press "Sign in with passkey", then use Deposit (Aurora quote and deposit address), Passkey (swap USDC to AUSD), Plant (preview, approve, deposit, plant; the 3D garden grows as simulated takers fill the bids), Hedge (open a Perpl short), the Gardener card on the Plant tab (set up a mandate and start it), and Board. Add ?autoplay=1 to the address to watch the whole flow press its own buttons.

5. Real money (a judge does not need it): on the main page, Deposit, Plant, Hedge and Passkey use a Mera passkey session on Monad mainnet (Face ID or Touch ID; Safari with iCloud Keychain, or Chrome with Google Password Manager). Caps: 15 USDC per deposit and per planting, 15 USD of hedge, 15 AUSD deposited; every transaction is simulated, announced in plain words and confirmed once.

6. Source code, 332 tests, every real transaction hash (docs/FINDINGS.md): https://github.com/OwenZhao9/flip-garden
