HOW TO TRY FLIP GARDEN (no account or login is needed to look around)

1. Open https://owenzhao9.github.io/flip-garden/ in any modern browser, on a phone or a computer.

2. Ledger tab (read-only, no wallet needed): press "Try the example wallet". It re-reads our own real-money test from Monad mainnet (blocks 112,209,462 to 112,211,600; about 15 to 25 seconds the first time) and should show "Matches the chain" with all 6 checks at a difference of 0, a cash change of -4.976961 USDC, a coin change of +201 MON and network fees of 0.095135 MON. You can also paste any Monad wallet address and a start block (the first block to read) to see that wallet's Kuru flip-order ledger.

3. Plant tab (uses your own browser wallet): connect a wallet on Monad mainnet that holds about 7 USDC and a little MON for network fees. The page never asks for a key; it simulates every transaction before the wallet pop-up and checks the result on chain afterwards; approvals are for the exact amount and the hard caps are 15 USDC per deposit and per planting. This flow has not yet been run with real money, so please use a throwaway wallet and a few dollars. A wallet is not required to judge the project; the real-money test is documented in the README.

4. Source code, tests and links to every real test transaction: https://github.com/OwenZhao9/flip-garden
