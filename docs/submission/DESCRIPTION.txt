FLIP GARDEN: plant a few dollars as Kuru flip orders on Monad, watch them grow, and check every number against the chain

WHAT IT IS
Kuru is a fully on-chain order book on Monad. A flip order is a limit order that, once filled, places the opposite order by itself, so every completed round trip earns the spread with no bot. Flip Garden turns this into a garden a beginner can follow: plant a few dollars as flip orders, sign in with a Mera passkey (Face ID or Touch ID, no wallet extension, no seed phrase), watch each order grow as a plant in a 3D garden, and read a ledger that reconciles every number with the chain and shows losses as clearly as gains.

WHY MONAD: MARKET MAKING IN FIVE-DOLLAR STEPS
Flip Garden is a market structure that only works with fast, cheap on-chain settlement: retail market making in five-dollar steps.
- Size: one price level is Kuru's minimum order, 200 MON, about $5.
- Margin: a flip bid one tick from the best price earns up to about 0.1% per completed round trip; on a $6 bid that is up to 0.006 USDC.
- Cost: the three transactions of a planting cost 0.065 MON in network fees on Monad, about 0.2 cents, and Kuru reads 0 bps maker fees on this market. Fees of a few cents per transaction would cost more than a round trip earns.
- Speed: our first bid filled about 0.6 seconds after it was placed and Kuru placed the flipped order in the same block, so the garden grows while the user watches.
- On chain end to end: order book, matching and flip run in Kuru's contracts; Flip Garden adds no off-chain matching, keeper or custody, and no custom contracts.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Plant: preview up to three flip bids, each transaction simulated from your address and confirmed once; exact approvals, hard caps of 15 USDC.
- Deposit: bring USDC from Base, Arbitrum, Ethereum, Optimism or Polygon with Aurora Intents.
- Grow: plots go from seed to flower to fruit with real fills; levels, achievements and a leaderboard ranked by round trips, never by profit.
- Prune, move, harvest, unwind: cancel, re-place or withdraw at any time.
- Hedge: a small short on Perpl's MON perpetual, paid in AUSD, with cost and liquidation estimate shown first.
- Gardener: an agent that tends the garden inside a mandate you confirm once, with fixed rules or the Jev model.
- Ledger: every fill, fee, gas cost and profit or loss, checked against on-chain balances. Account history is read through Envio HyperSync.
