FLIP GARDEN: plant a few dollars as Kuru flip orders on Monad, hedge the leftover coins, and check every number against the chain

WHAT IT IS
Flip Garden turns a hard-to-read DeFi mechanic into a garden a beginner can follow. Kuru is a fully on-chain order book on Monad. A flip order is a limit order that, once filled, automatically places the opposite order at a price chosen in advance, so every completed round trip earns the spread with no bot or keeper. Flip Garden lets anyone plant a few dollars as flip orders, sign in with a Mera passkey (Face ID or Touch ID; the passkey is the only account layer, no browser wallet needed), deposit USDC from other chains with Aurora Intents, watch the garden grow in 3D, prune, move or harvest, hedge the MON the orders pile up with a short on Perpl, let the Gardener agent tend the garden inside a mandate you confirm once, and read a ledger in which every number is reconciled against on-chain balances and losses are shown as clearly as gains.

THE GARDEN
- Plant: choose a small budget; the page previews up to three flip bids, simulates every transaction from your address and asks for one explicit confirmation per transaction. Approvals are for the exact amount; hard caps are 15 USDC per deposit and per planting.
- Deposit: bring USDC from Base, Arbitrum, Ethereum, Optimism or Polygon to your Monad account with Aurora Intents; the page asks Aurora for a quote and a one-time deposit address and re-reads your Monad balance when the funds arrive.
- Grow: each order is a plot that grows with real fills (seed, sprout, flower, and fruit for a completed round trip), shown as a 3D garden (Three.js) with an SVG fallback. The garden has a level (Seed, Sprout, Bloom, Orchard), achievements, and a leaderboard ranked by completed round trips and garden age, never by profit.
- Prune, move, harvest: cancel orders, move one to a new price, or withdraw the free balance at Kuru.
- Hedge: open a small short on Perpl's MON perpetual against the MON your flip bids collect, see the cost, risk and liquidation estimate first, and close it any time.
- Gardener: you confirm a mandate once (budget, allowed actions, expiry, limits per hour); inside it the Gardener re-places or prunes bids that left the price range, plants free USDC again, harvests idle money, keeps MON for network fees and adjusts the hedge. Decisions come from fixed rules or from Jev (an AI model reached through the leaderboard Worker); every action passes the page's guard and lands in a timeline.
- Ledger: balances, open orders, flip chains, every fill, fees, gas, profit and loss, with a badge saying whether the ledger matches the chain.

WHAT HAS RUN ON MONAD MAINNET WITH REAL FUNDS
2026-10-10, command line: one flip bid filled completely about 0.6 s after it was placed, Kuru created the flipped sell in the same block, we cancelled and withdrew, and the ledger matched the chain with a difference of 0 (five transactions, 0.095135 MON of fees).
2026-10-11, in the browser: the planting flow with MetaMask (7 transactions, reconciled), then one round with a passkey account: 12 USDC swapped to 12.001173 AUSD through Kuru Flow; a bid planted through the passkey signer that filled in the next block and flipped by itself; prune and harvest; a Perpl hedge (a 219 MON short filled completely, closed, and the collateral withdrawn; total cost 0.034756 AUSD, the sum of the price result, fees and funding shown on the page); the wallet listed on the live leaderboard; and a final ledger with 7 checks at a difference of 0.
Every hash and number is in docs/FINDINGS.md in the repository, and the real data is a test fixture that 332 passing tests assert. There are no custom smart contracts: everything uses Kuru's, Perpl's and Kuru Flow's own contracts. A leaderboard service (a small read-only Cloudflare Worker) recomputes figures from public chain data; it holds no key and sends no transaction.

HOW IT IS BUILT
- A dependency-free TypeScript ledger core: event decoding, flip-order chains, free and locked balances, fills, fees, gas, realized and unrealized profit and loss, reconciliation and a resumable log scanner. All money math uses exact integers.
- Every transaction the page can send is decoded and checked against a short allow-list, simulated from the user's address, announced in plain words, confirmed once, and verified on chain afterwards.
- Passkey sign-in through Mera: the key exists only in memory while you are signed in.
- History for old wallets through Envio HyperSync, behind the leaderboard Worker.
- A web app (Ledger, Deposit, Plant, Hedge, Board and Passkey tabs) that installs to the home screen, plus a demo page (demo.html) that runs the same pages against a simulated chain, so the whole signed-in flow can be tried without money.

WHAT WE LEARNED ON-CHAIN
- The minimum order on this market is 200 MON (about $5), so each price level costs at least about $5; the garden suits budgets of roughly $10 to $100.
- In Kuru's trade events, isBuy describes the taker's side. A filled flip order produces a new order id, so the ledger follows chains of ids.
- Monad charges gas by the gas limit, not by gas used.
- Perpl's exchange accepts orders straight from a web page, so the hedge needs no relay server.

WHY MONAD
Fills show up within a second or two, so watching a garden grow feels alive; fees are a fraction of a cent, so a small garden is not eaten by costs; and Kuru runs a fully on-chain order book with native flip orders, so there is nothing to build off-chain.
