Milestone: the app now covers the whole path from another chain to a managed garden. Deposit from Base, Arbitrum, Ethereum, Optimism or Polygon with Aurora Intents; Mera passkey sign-in as the only account layer; a 3D garden (Three.js) that grows with real fills; the Gardener agent, which tends the garden inside a mandate the user confirms once (decisions from fixed rules or from Jev); history for old wallets through Envio HyperSync. A demo page (demo.html) runs the same pages against a simulated chain so the signed-in flow can be tried without money. Technical demo video and pitch video are linked on the submission. 332 tests pass; source: https://github.com/OwenZhao9/flip-garden\n
