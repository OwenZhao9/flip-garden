# Pitch video: atmosphere shots from a video model

Five short shots for the pitch video only. They carry mood, never product facts: no interface, no numbers, no text, no logos. The product itself is shown with real screen recordings.

Settings for every shot: 16:9, 1920x1080, 5 to 6 seconds, 24 fps, no audio, slow camera motion, seamless start and end so the editor can cut in and out.

## Consistency block (paste at the end of every prompt)

Style: soft cinematic 3D, cosy low-poly game art with smooth rounded shapes, gentle volumetric light, shallow depth of field. Palette: deep violet-black background (#14102B), dark purple surfaces (#1E1840), glowing lime green (#9BE564) for living things, soft purple light (#836EF9), small warm amber accents. Calm, hopeful, magical, premium. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no user interface, no watermark, no people's faces.

## Negative list (if the tool has a negative prompt field)

text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, user interface, screen content, charts, faces, hands close-up, realistic photo, harsh light, clutter, fast camera, flicker, distortion

## Shot 1: opening, a seed wakes up (0:00)

A single small seed resting in a mound of dark soil on a floating island of earth in a violet night sky. A thin ring of purple light pulses around it, then the seed cracks open and a tiny lime-green sprout rises and unfolds two leaves. Tiny glowing particles drift upward. Slow push-in camera.

## Shot 2: the problem, a maze of signals (about 0:12)

A lone small sprout in a pot sits in the centre of a vast dark space filled with fast flowing streams of abstract purple and amber light lines that cross and tangle like a busy market, too fast to follow. The sprout's leaves droop slightly. The camera slowly pulls back to show how large and chaotic the space is. Mood: overwhelming but not scary.

## Shot 3: the garden grows (about 0:40)

A neat round garden bed on a floating island at dusk. Plants grow in a time-lapse: seeds become sprouts, sprouts become lime-green flowers, one becomes a small tree with glowing amber fruits. Each plant sways gently at its own rhythm. Soft fireflies. Slow orbit around the bed.

## Shot 4: the gardener tends at night (about 1:10)

A small friendly glowing orb of purple light floats calmly between the plants of the garden bed at night, pausing above each plant; where it pauses, a plant straightens or a dry leaf falls away. Calm, careful, protective. Slow tracking shot following the orb.

## Shot 5: closing, a thriving floating garden (about 1:50)

Wide shot of the floating island with a full, healthy garden of lime-green plants and small fruit trees under a violet sky, gentle light rising from the bed, particles drifting up into the stars. The camera rises slowly and the image fades to deep violet-black, leaving space in the centre for a title added later.
