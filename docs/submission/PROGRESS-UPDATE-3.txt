Milestone: planting is in. The live page https://owenzhao9.github.io/flip-garden/ now has two views. Ledger (read-only) still reproduces our real-money test with a difference of 0. Plant lets a visitor connect their own wallet, preview up to three flip bids, then approve, deposit and plant with one wallet pop-up per step. Every call is checked against a short allow-list, simulated from the visitor's address before the pop-up and verified on chain afterwards; approvals are exact and the hard caps are 15 USDC. It is built and tested with a fake wallet and chain (121 tests) but has not yet been run with real money; that first run is next, and the page says so. Also confirmed read-only on mainnet: a web page can place Perpl orders directly on chain, so no relay server is needed (17 simulations and 10 failure controls behaved as expected; nothing was sent). Next: passkey sign-in, an AUSD balance, a Perpl hedge for the MON that flip orders accumulate, and a visual redesign.
